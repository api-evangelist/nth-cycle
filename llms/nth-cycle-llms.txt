# Nth Cycle

> Nth Cycle is a critical mineral refining company headquartered in Beverly, Massachusetts. It
> recovers production-grade nickel, cobalt, copper and rare earth elements from mined ore, low-grade
> tailings and recycled industrial scrap using OYSTER, its patented modular electro-extraction
> platform. Nth Cycle does not publish a public API, developer portal, SDK, or machine-readable
> contract of any kind — it is a materials refining company, not an API provider.

## About

- [Nth Cycle](https://nthcycle.com/): Company home — critical mineral refining for batteries, magnets and semiconductors.
- [The Platform](https://nthcycle.com/the-platform): OYSTER, a modular electro-extraction system combining electrochemistry, filtration and chemical precipitation in one continuous closed-loop architecture — roughly 70% lower capital intensity, 75% less waste, viable at 5-10x smaller scale than a centralized refinery.
- [Why Refining Matters](https://nthcycle.com/why-refining-matters): The critical mineral supply-chain gap Nth Cycle targets.
- [Team](https://nthcycle.com/team): Leadership and technical team.
- [Careers](https://nthcycle.com/careers): Open roles.
- [Contact](https://nthcycle.com/contact): Primary contact channel.

## Markets

- [Battery Materials](https://nthcycle.com/battery-materials): Nickel, cobalt and related battery feedstocks, including premium domestic MHP.
- [Rare Earth Elements](https://nthcycle.com/rare-earth-elements): Rare earth refining, including the Ionic Rare Earths partnership.
- [Copper](https://nthcycle.com/copper): Copper recovery and refining.
